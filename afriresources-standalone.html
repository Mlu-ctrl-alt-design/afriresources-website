01 / About

About
AfriResources.

AfriResources is a South African commodity trading and mining services business, sourcing coal, chrome and manganese from producers across Southern Africa and delivering them into domestic and export markets.

We work across the full commercial chain — securing product at the mine, managing quality and beneficiation to customer specification, and coordinating road, rail and export logistics through established transport partners. That integration is what allows us to take responsibility for a load from face to customer, rather than for one leg of its journey.

The business is built on long-term commercial relationships, documented operational procedures and disciplined reconciliation — the foundations that let producers, industrial customers and international trading partners rely on us at scale.
