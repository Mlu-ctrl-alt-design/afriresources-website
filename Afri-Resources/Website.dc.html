Who We Are
We are a 100% Black-owned bulk commodity trading company.

AfriResources is a South African-based, 100% Black-owned bulk commodity trading and mineral processing company. We specialise in coal, chrome, and manganese for both export and domestic markets.

We are building deep operational expertise across the full mineral value chain — from sourcing and quality control to logistics and final delivery.

Mission

To establish a sustainable business navigating different market conditions with a global footprint, maintaining an untarnished record for excellence.

Vision

To be Africa's leading bulk commodity and mineral processing company, transforming the way bulk commodity trading is done globally.
