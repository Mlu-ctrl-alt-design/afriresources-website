01 / About

Built for the way commodities actually move.

{{ f.label }} {{ f.value }}

AfriResources is a 100% black-owned commodity trading, mining services and logistics company operating across Southern Africa.

We source bulk minerals directly from established mining operations, manage quality and beneficiation to customer specification, and coordinate road, rail and export logistics through a network of trusted partners. Our customers span domestic industrial users, utilities and international trading houses.

The business is deliberately built as a single commercial thread — sourcing, quality, trading and logistics under one accountable team — so that counterparties deal with one point of contact from mine to delivery. That structure is what allows us to move product reliably at a scale usually associated with much larger houses, while keeping the responsiveness of an owner-led operation.

Coal remains our largest traded volume, alongside a growing chrome and manganese book, supported by documented governance, KYC onboarding and commercial structures suitable for institutional funders and export partners.
