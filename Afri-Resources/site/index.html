BM
Bongani Mthethwa
Chief Executive Officer

Bongani Mthethwa is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of AfriResources, providing the strategic vision and leadership behind the company's growth into an integrated mining, commodity trading, and logistics business.

With extensive experience across coal procurement, commodity trading, project development, and commercial negotiations, Bongani has built a strong network of mining partners, logistics providers, financiers, and industrial customers throughout Southern Africa. He leads the company’s growth strategy, strategic partnerships, capital raising initiatives, and expansion into both domestic and international markets.

His leadership is centred on delivering innovative, reliable, and sustainable commodity solutions while positioning AfriResources as a trusted partner in Africa’s mining and energy sectors.

TD
Thulani K. Dhlamini
Chief Operating Officer

Thulani K. Dhlamini is the Chief Operating Officer of AfriResources, responsible for leading the company’s operations, supply chain, logistics, and commercial execution across Southern Africa.

With over a decade of experience in finance, supply chain management, and cross-border logistics, Thulani has held leadership roles at Samsung SDS South Africa and Eaton Electric South Africa, where he successfully delivered operational improvement and financial governance initiatives. His expertise spans commodity trading, process optimisation, automation, and operational strategy.

At AfriResources, he is focused on building scalable operational systems, strengthening governance, and driving sustainable growth across the company’s mining and commodity trading activities.
